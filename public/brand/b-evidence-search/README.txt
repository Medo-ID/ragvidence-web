B. Evidence search
Retrieval reveals the verified fragment among document chunks.

Construction: Two document fragments enter a circular search aperture; a visible check occupies the lens and the diagonal handle completes the retrieval silhouette.
16px adaptation: The lens, handle, and check are snapped to a dedicated 16px drawing with shorter fragment tabs.

Use logo-dark/mark-dark on dark surfaces; light variants on off-white. Black and white variants are one-color transparent artwork. SVGs have editable vector paths and outlined Geist lettering. PNG logo/mark exports are transparent. Favicon and app exports intentionally have a solid Abyssal Teal field.

RAGvidence is the exact wordmark capitalization. See ../brand-sheet.html for use guidance.
