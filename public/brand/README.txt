RAGvidence — three logo and favicon systems

A. Evidence convergence: fragments converge on a verified evidence point.
B. Evidence search: a search aperture reveals a checked match among fragments.
C. Connected V: retrieved chunks and a path resolve into a validation V.

Recommended: A, for the clearest combination of traceability and verification.
C has the simplest compact silhouette; B makes search most immediately familiar.
These are design judgments, not retrieval performance claims.

Open concepts.html for dark/light wordmarks, monochrome icons, native-size
favicons, construction, meaning, tradeoffs, and the five-criterion comparison.
Open brand-sheet.html or brand-sheet.pdf for colors, clear space, minimum size,
and incorrect uses. browser-tabs-dark.png and browser-tabs-light.png are actual
Chrome UI captures at 1x scale, with all three dedicated 16px icons.

Each concept folder contains:
- Horizontal logo: dark, light, pure black, pure white (SVG and transparent PNG)
- Icon-only mark: dark, light, pure black, pure white (SVG and transparent PNG)
- Separate 16, 32, 48px favicon drawings (SVG and PNG), plus a multi-frame ICO
- Simplified scalable favicon SVG
- 180px Apple touch icon; 192px and 512px app icons
- Browser-tab preview and usage notes

The source folder in the complete archive contains editable vector masters and
outlined Geist wordmark paths. No remote fonts, stock icons, gradients, shadows,
or embedded raster images appear in the SVG artwork. Geist licensing is included.

Use light variants on off-white and dark variants on near-black. The favicon/app
assets intentionally use a solid Abyssal Teal field; logo/mark PNGs are transparent.
The verification motif is brand meaning, not a product pass/fail badge.

The website now uses concept A in its header, footer, and app/browser icons.
